MoveMouse Shipping

The first piece of Secretary to ship on its own. Teaching an assistant to drive a PC meant first solving how a phone talks to one — and that part turned out to be worth releasing by itself. MoveMouse turns your Android phone into a wireless Bluetooth mouse, keyboard and mouse jiggler for any PC — no drivers, no dongle, just pair and control.

About us

Cool Artifacts SAS is an AI startup based in Viroflay, France, on a mission to empower people in the age of AI. We build across two lines: Secretary, an AI assistant that perceives and helps operate your devices — MoveMouse, which turns a phone into a wireless controller for your PC, is its first spinoff to ship — and Trameo, a generative-film platform reinventing how people communicate and share ideas, through living, AI-generated audiovisual content.

Both lines are the same engineering problem twice over: running inference on hardware we do not own, under a budget we do not set.
